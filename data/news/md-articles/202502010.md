# SJA server migration notice

## Happy Lunar New Year

Thank you for using SJA. The service has been running for four years, including more than a year on the web. As a developer influenced by Scratch, I am glad to contribute to its community.

My studies have delayed features such as archived reports and project comparison. At the time of this announcement, I planned to revisit them at the end of 2025.

By January 25, 2025, SJA had generated more than 6,300 reports. Thank you for your support. Please contact me by email with questions.
